## Code and benchmarks

<dl class="resource-list">
  <dt><a href="https://github.com/panhomyoung/phyLS">PhyLS</a></dt>
  <dd>Logic synthesis with physical information, learning, and optimization. <a href="https://phyls.readthedocs.io/en/latest/">Documentation</a>.</dd>
  <dt><a href="https://github.com/krzhu/abcRL">abcRL</a></dt>
  <dd>Reinforcement learning and graph convolutional networks for logic optimization.</dd>
  <dt><a href="https://github.com/CODA-Team/AnalogGym">AnalogGym</a></dt>
  <dd>A benchmark suite for circuit sizing and optimization.</dd>
  <dt><a href="https://github.com/magical-eda/MAGICAL">MAGICAL</a></dt>
  <dd>An automated analog and mixed-signal layout system, from netlist to GDSII.</dd>
</dl>
